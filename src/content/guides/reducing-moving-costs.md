## Start with an accurate removal quote

The biggest cost mistake is booking based on a guess. Online calculators give a useful starting point (try our [moving cost calculator](/moving-cost-calculator/)), but a fixed quote after survey reflects your actual volume, access and any special requirements.

### Why surveys save money

An in-person or video survey identifies items needing dismantling and reassembly, parking restrictions on Wigan terraces, and volume that might otherwise trigger surprise charges. You receive one agreed price for the agreed service. No hourly clock running while the crew navigates a narrow stairwell in Scholes or a tight bay near Standishgate.

When comparing removal quotes, check what each includes: number of crew, van size, insurance levels, packing, and furniture dismantling. A cheaper quote without goods in transit cover is not cheaper if something breaks.

## Declutter before you pack

Every item you move costs time, space and labour during loading and unloading. Decluttering is the single most effective way to reduce moving costs without affecting service quality.

### Room-by-room approach

Work through each room and sort items into keep, sell, donate and discard. Be ruthless with loft contents, duplicate kitchen gadgets, old furniture and clothes you have not worn in a year. Sell valuable pieces online; donate usable goods to local charity shops.

Less volume means a smaller time estimate, fewer packing materials, and potentially a lower fixed quote at survey.

Important: we are not a waste clearance company. Do not expect the removal van to take bags of rubbish, broken furniture for dumping, or garden waste. Arrange disposal separately before moving day so the crew only loads what is going to the new house.

## Choose the right moving date

If your solicitor or landlord will allow it, a midweek slot is usually easier to book than the last Friday. We quote the job, not the day of the week, but diary choice is better away from month-end. Longer Greater Manchester runs still benefit from an early start to miss peak traffic on the M6, M61 and A580.

## Pack yourself, but pack properly

Self-packing is a legitimate saving on labour, provided you use decent boxes and protect fragiles. Borrowing supermarket cartons for towels is fine; using them for crockery is how you pay twice.

### Cost-conscious packing

- Buy or hire double-wall boxes for books, kitchenware and anything heavy
- Use small boxes for dense items so loading and unloading stays safe
- Label by room to cut crew time at the new address
- Book fragile-only packing if you only want professionals on glass and china

Professionally packed fragiles are eligible for breakage cover under our terms; self-packed boxes are not. If your kitchen is full of wedding china, paying for packing on that room can be cheaper than a replacement dinner service.

## Prepare the house so the crew is not delayed

Quotes assume you are ready when the team arrives. Time spent waiting while you empty wardrobes or hunt for parking is time that belongs in the plan, or, if the job was scoped on a ready house, a reason extras appear with less organised operators.

### Free preparation that protects the quote

- Defrost the freezer the day before
- Disconnect appliances as required in the terms
- Dismantle simple flat-pack if you are comfortable doing so, or book it in so it is priced
- Clear driveways and hallways
- Apply for a Wigan Council parking dispensation if the Luton cannot legally stand outside
- Keep jewellery, documents and cash with you

Parking tickets and second trips because a van could not stop are expensive compared with a dispensation arranged a week earlier.

## Compare like with like

When you collect quotes, write down:

- Survey method (in person, video, or none)
- Insurance: goods in transit amount and public liability
- Whether packing, dismantling and reassembly are included
- Van type and whether the same crew does loading and unloading
- What happens if volume was under-declared

Warrior Removals is not a man-and-van-only service. You get a surveyed, insured domestic removal with a written price. That costs more than an uninsured cash job, and it should: you are paying for cover, crew training and a van that is the right size.

## Access issues that inflate cost

Wigan has plenty of straightforward houses with drives in Standish, Winstanley and Hawkley Hall. It also has terraces, flats above shops, and streets where a 3.5-tonne Luton cannot park outside the door. Long carries, extra flights of stairs and waiting for lifts all affect labour.

Tell us at survey. Surprises on the day are what generate extras. Honest information keeps the fixed quote honest.

## Where not to cut

Do not drop insurance to save a few pounds. Do not skip the survey. Do not move a piano, American fridge or glass table on a borrowed trailer. Do not overload a small van for a second illegal trip. Those shortcuts are how a “cheap” move becomes the most expensive weekend of the year.

## A realistic budget-move formula

Declutter hard, pack clothes and books yourself with proper boxes, prep parking and appliances, move on a date that is not the busiest Friday if you can, and spend the rest of the budget on an insured professional crew for loading, transit and unloading. That split captures most of the saving without the horror stories.
