## Appoint a move coordinator

Every office relocation needs one person with authority to make decisions. The move coordinator liaises with the removal company, IT support, landlords and staff.

### Responsibilities

- Maintain the master relocation timeline
- Book the removal survey and confirm the fixed quote
- Communicate move dates and packing instructions to all employees
- Coordinate access at both old and new premises
- Manage change-of-address notifications for clients, suppliers and regulators
- Confirm parking, loading bays and lift bookings

A single point of contact prevents mixed instructions on moving day, which is the usual cause of delays during loading and unloading.

## Book a commercial survey early

Do not price an office move from a bedroom count or a quick phone guess. Commercial volume, desk runs, IT cabinets, archive boxes and access constraints only become clear on survey.

Warrior Removals offers in-person or video surveys. We look at floor plans, stairs versus lifts, loading bays, parking on streets such as Standishgate, and whether workstations need dismantling and reassembly. Use the [moving cost calculator](/moving-cost-calculator/) for a ballpark figure, then treat the survey quote as the figure you budget against.

### What the survey should capture

- Headcount of desks, chairs, pedestals and meeting tables
- IT equipment: screens, towers, printers, server hardware
- Archive volume and whether files can be packed in advance
- Kitchen and reception furniture
- Restricted hours, out-of-hours loading, and neighbour or landlord rules
- Floor protection needs on carpeted or newly fitted offices

## Build an inventory before packing starts

Photograph and list furniture and equipment before anything is boxed. Label cables, tag monitors to the desks they belong with, and decide what is moving versus what will be sold or disposed of separately. Warrior Removals moves office belongings to the new address; we do not take waste, old chairs for dumping, or general clearance.

An inventory speeds unpacking and helps you check the load at both ends. It also stops last-minute “can you take this as well?” requests that change van space on the day.

## IT, phones and data

Treat IT as its own mini-project, not an afterthought on Friday afternoon.

### Practical IT steps

- Agree a freeze date for non-essential system changes
- Back up servers and laptops before disconnection
- Label every cable and photograph rack layouts
- Confirm broadband go-live at the new site **before** the physical move
- Plan who reconnects kit: your IT provider, not the removal crew, unless quoted otherwise
- Pack monitors and towers in original packaging where you still have it, or use proper packing materials

Confidential files should travel in sealed, labelled cartons. Do not leave personnel records in open crates in a shared corridor during loading.

## Staff communication and personal packing

Give staff a written timetable: what they must pack, what stays on desks until the last day, and where personal items should go. Provide packing materials for drawer contents and ask people to take plants, food and valuables home. We do not move plants, perishable food or personal high-value items.

If you want professional packing for kitchens, meeting rooms and IT, book it as part of the same quote. Packing the day before the move keeps moving day focused on loading and unloading rather than boxing mugs while the van waits.

## Legal and administrative tasks

An office relocation triggers paperwork beyond the physical move.

- Change of address with Companies House, HMRC, banks and insurers
- Update website, email footers and Google Business Profile
- Notify clients and suppliers with an effective date
- Transfer electricity, gas, water and broadband, and arrange waste collection with the correct contractor, separately from the removal
- Review lease obligations on the old premises, including dilapidations
- Update fire safety and health and safety documentation for the new layout

Allow several weeks for registrations and utility transfers in Greater Manchester. We do not provide international office shipping.

## Post-move setup

The relocation is not finished when the last pedestal is unloaded.

- IT fully operational before staff return
- Health and safety walkthrough: fire exits, first aid, display screen setup
- Furniture reassembly: desks, meeting tables and reception seating in place
- Signage updated on directories and doors
- Staff orientation covering facilities, parking and emergency procedures

A calm first morning in the new office is the real test of the plan. Work backwards from that morning: coordinator and survey at eight to twelve weeks, IT and broadband at six, staff packing at two, final walkthrough in the last week.
