## Eight weeks before moving day

### Confirm dates and book your removal company

Contact your solicitor, estate agent or landlord to confirm exchange, completion or key-handover dates. Once you have a firm moving date, request quotes from reputable local firms. ### Start decluttering

Go through each room and decide what is moving with you. Sell, donate or arrange separate disposal of items you no longer need. Warrior Removals is a removals company, not a waste clearance service: the van is loaded only with belongings going to your new home. Decluttering before packing reduces volume, which can lower your quote and simplify loading and unloading at both properties.

### Notify key organisations

Inform your employer, GP surgery, dentist, bank and insurance providers of your change of address. If you have children, contact their schools early. Register with Wigan Council for council tax at your new address and arrange a final reading for utilities at your current home. Update your driving licence with the DVLA when you have a confirmed postcode.

## Four to six weeks before moving day

### Gather packing materials

Order packing materials early: sturdy double-wall boxes in mixed sizes, bubble wrap, packing paper, tape, marker pens and wardrobe cartons for hanging clothes. Label every box with its destination room and a brief contents note. Keep fragile items together and mark boxes clearly.

If you prefer not to pack yourself, Warrior Removals packing services supply professional-grade materials and trained packers who know how to protect glass, electronics and furniture for transit. Professional packing is usually completed the day before the move so loading and unloading can start promptly.

### Arrange change of address services

Set up Royal Mail redirection so post sent to your old address is not missed during the transition. Amend vehicle registration if applicable. Notify TV Licensing, subscriptions and online retailers. If you are moving into a different [area of Wigan](/areas/), note local parking rules and nearest shops before moving day.

### Plan access at both properties

Walk through loading and unloading logistics at your current and new home. Note narrow hallways, stairs, lift access and parking restrictions. Terraced streets around Scholes, Ince and Wigan town centre often need a Wigan Council parking dispensation for a Luton van. Flag any items that require dismantling and reassembly (beds, wardrobes, dining tables and flat-pack furniture) so your removal team can allow time and bring the right tools.

## Two to three weeks before moving day

### Pack room by room

Start with rooms you use least: loft, spare bedroom, garage and shed. Pack books flat in small boxes to keep weight manageable. Leave everyday kitchen items until the final week. Photograph cable setups behind televisions and computers before disconnecting them.

Moving day is busy. Arrange for children and pets to stay with friends or family if possible. This keeps them safe and lets the crew work efficiently during loading and unloading. Pets are not transported as part of house removal services.

## One week before moving day

### Finish packing non-essentials

Aim to have most boxes packed by midweek. Prepare a first-night box with kettle, mugs, tea, snacks, phone chargers, toiletries, bedding, a torch and basic tools. Keep passports, completion documents, keys and medication in a bag you carry personally.

### Defrost, clean and finish utilities

Defrost your freezer at least 24 hours before the move. Clean appliances you are leaving if the sale or tenancy requires it. Take meter readings and photograph them. Disconnect washing machines and dishwashers as set out in your removal terms. Confirm broadband at the new address and check bin days if you are changing local authority.

## Moving day checklist

### Before the crew arrives

Strip beds except the one you slept in. Do a final sweep of cupboards, loft and garden outbuildings. Keep paths clear for loading and unloading. Protect floors and banisters if needed.

When Warrior Removals arrives, walk the team through both properties. Point out fragile items, boxes marked “open first” and anything needing extra care. The crew will wrap furniture with blankets and stretch wrap, then load systematically into a 3.5-tonne Luton van, using straps so nothing shifts in transit.

### During the move

Stay reachable by phone. Be present at the new property before unloading begins so you can direct boxes to the correct rooms. Check items against your inventory if you prepared one. Test smoke alarms and locate the stopcock and consumer unit at the new house.

### After unloading

Inspect furniture and boxes for damage before the crew leaves. Sign paperwork only when you are satisfied. Assemble beds and essential furniture first. Take meter readings at the new property. Register with a local GP if you have moved into a new catchment.
