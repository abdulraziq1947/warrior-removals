## What to take and what to leave

Student rooms accumulate clutter fast. Before packing, decide what is worth transporting.

### Usually worth moving

- Laptops, monitors and electronics (pack these as fragiles)
- Bedding, towels and clothes
- Kitchen essentials if moving into an unfurnished house share
- Textbooks and course materials for next semester
- Small furniture you own: desk chair, lamp, mini fridge if allowed

### Often better to replace

- Half-used toiletries and cleaning products
- Old notes you will never reread
- Broken or duplicate kitchen items
- Flat-pack furniture that will not survive another assembly

Selling textbooks or passing pans to the incoming tenant reduces volume and can lower your removal quote. We move what you are keeping; we do not take bin bags of rubbish as a waste run.

## Packing tips for student moves

You do not need a warehouse of materials, but you do need enough boxes and proper protection for electronics.

### Practical packing advice

- **Use small boxes for books**: a large box of textbooks is unsafe to lift
- **Wrap electronics** in clothing or bubble wrap; pack cables in labelled bags
- **Bag hanging clothes** in bin liners with hangers poking through, or use wardrobe boxes
- **Label every box** with your name, new address and room (Bedroom, Kitchen)
- **Keep valuables with you**: passport, laptop, phone, wallet and medication travel in your bag or car, not on the van

## Moving out of halls or college residences

Halls and managed blocks often have strict checkout times and limited parking for loading. Check the moving-out checklist from your accommodation team:

- Book a loading bay or parking slot if required
- Schedule the crew after your final room inspection where possible
- Clean the room to protect your deposit, removal day is not an excuse to leave mess
- Confirm lift access and whether trolleys are allowed in corridors
- Photograph the empty room once the van is loaded

## Moving into a shared house

House shares near the college, in Leigh town centre, Hindley, Platt Bridge and Wigan town centre, often involve narrow stairs, limited parking and several tenants arriving on the same day.

### Before moving in

- Confirm your tenancy start date and key collection time
- Photograph the property condition for your deposit record
- Check which furniture the landlord provides, do not move a sofa you do not need
- Coordinate with housemates so four vans are not blocking the same street at 10am

### Access and parking

Terraced streets around Scholes, Ince and the town centre rarely have driveways. Arrange parking with housemates or apply for a temporary dispensation through Wigan Council if you are loading or unloading on restricted kerbs. Tell your removal company about stairs and narrow doorways at survey so the quote includes an adequate crew.
