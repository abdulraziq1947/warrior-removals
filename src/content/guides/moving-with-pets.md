Moving with pets in Wigan requires planning your animal’s safety separately from the furniture move: update microchip and vet details, pack a pet essentials bag (food, water, bowls, medication, bedding, lead and carrier), keep pets away from the property during loading and unloading, transport cats in secure carriers and dogs on harnesses in the car, and register with a new vet before moving day. Keeping pets out of the way during dismantling and reassembly protects them from open doors, heavy furniture and stressed behaviour.

Pets pick up on household disruption long before the removal van arrives. Boxes, changed routines and unfamiliar people in the home cause anxiety for dogs, cats, rabbits and caged birds alike. ## Before moving day: veterinary and admin tasks

Start pet-related admin at least two weeks before your Wigan move.

### Update records and registrations

- **Microchip details**: contact the database (Petlog, Identibase or others) with your new address on moving day, not weeks later
- **Veterinary registration**: research and register with a practice near your new home in Wigan, Standish, Leigh or wherever you are heading in Greater Manchester; ask your current vet for vaccination records and medical history
- **Pet insurance**: notify your insurer of the address change and confirm cover continues in the new postcode
- **Identification tags**: update collar tags with a mobile number that will work on the day

Wigan and the surrounding boroughs have plenty of veterinary practices. Having one lined up before an emergency arises is essential, especially if your pet is on long-term medication.

Do not ask a vet for a sedative “just in case” without a proper discussion. Many animals travel better calm, familiar and contained than chemically drowsy. Follow veterinary advice for your individual pet.

## Prepare a pet moving-day kit

Pack a separate bag for your pet that travels **with you**, never on the removal van with the rest of your goods in transit.

### What to include

- **Food and treats** for at least three days, sudden diet changes cause stomach upset
- **Water bowl and bottled water**: tap water can taste different in a new area
- **Medication**, flea and wormer treatments, and a copy of vaccination records
- **Litter, trays or puppy pads** plus waste bags
- **Familiar bedding and a well-used toy** that smells of home
- **Lead, harness, muzzle if used, and a secure carrier**
- **Cleaning wipes** for accidents in the car
- **Recent photograph** on your phone in case they slip out of a door

Label the bag clearly so nobody “helpfully” loads it onto the Luton during packing.

## Keep pets away from loading and unloading

Moving day is the highest-risk window. Front doors stay open, furniture blocks escape routes, and a startled cat can vanish under a neighbour’s car in seconds.

### Practical options

- Book a cattery, kennels or a trusted friend for the day
- Set up a **quiet closed room** with water, litter and bedding, with a clear sign on the door, and tell the crew not to enter
- Take dogs out for a long walk before the van arrives, then keep them in the car or with a helper away from the property

Never leave a pet loose in a house full of open doors. Never shut an animal in a room the crew needs to empty. Coordinate with Warrior Removals so the team knows which door is out of bounds.

If you must be at both addresses, have a second person whose only job is the pet. House removal services run more smoothly when the crew is not also acting as an unofficial dog-sitter.

## Travelling between properties

Most local Wigan moves are short: town centre to Swinley, Hindley to Aspull, Leigh to Atherton. Even a twenty-minute hop is stressful if the animal is unrestrained.

### Dogs

Use a crash-tested crate or a harness clipped to a seatbelt. Do not let a dog ride loose in a footwell. Plan toilet stops if you are heading further across Greater Manchester. Keep the same feeding time if you can; delay a big meal until after you arrive if your dog is travel-sick.

### Cats

Cats belong in a secure carrier, not on a lap. Cover the carrier with a light towel to reduce visual stress, but keep ventilation clear. Do not open the carrier “for a stroke” on the driveway of the new house, that is how indoor cats bolt.

### Small animals

Rabbits, guinea pigs and birds need stable, well-ventilated carriers in the car, out of direct sun and away from heater vents. Check they have travelled before moving day if possible. Do not load hutches onto the van with animals still inside.

## Settling into the new home

When you arrive, set up one quiet room first: litter tray or puppy pads, water, bedding, hiding places. Let cats explore that room before the rest of the house. Walk the garden and check fencing before dogs go out unattended, terraces in Scholes, Ince and Pemberton often have low walls and shared alleys.

Keep feeding and walk times as close as possible to the old routine. Unpack the pet kit before you start on the kitchen. A familiar bowl on the floor does more for anxiety than a perfectly arranged lounge.

If you have moved into a new [part of Wigan](/areas/), spend a few quiet days on local walking routes, Mesnes Park from Swinley, canal towpaths, or lanes around Shevington and Orrell, before busy high streets.

Leave beds and feeding stations until last while you pack, and tell professional packers which room the pet is in.
