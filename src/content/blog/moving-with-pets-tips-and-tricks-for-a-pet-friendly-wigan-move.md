A house move is noisy, smelly (to a dog) and full of strangers walking off with the sofa. If you are planning domestic removals in Wigan with a cat, dog or small pet, treat their plan as a separate job from the furniture. The crew can handle packing, loading and unloading of your belongings. You handle the carrier, the lead and the closed door.

Warrior Removals does not transport animals. That is a feature, not a gap: pets are safer in your car, with familiar people, than in a Luton van full of wardrobes. Here is how to keep the day calm for them and efficient for the team.

## Do the admin before the boxes appear

A fortnight before moving day, book a routine vet check if vaccinations or medication need updating. Ask for a printed history you can take to a new practice in Standish, Leigh, Hindley or wherever you are heading. Update collar tags with a mobile number that will work on the day.

Change the microchip address when you have the new keys, not a month later. If your pet slips a collar on an unfamiliar street in [another part of Wigan](/areas/), the chip is what gets them home.

Skip “just in case” sedatives unless your vet has prescribed them for this animal. Most pets do better with routine, a dark carrier and a quiet room than with a groggy first night in a new house.

## Build a kit that never goes on the van

Pack a dedicated bag: two or three days of food, bottled water, bowls, medication, litter or waste bags, a worn blanket, a toy that already smells of home, lead, harness and a spare photo on your phone. Keep that bag in the car or with you from the night before.

Well-meaning helpers load anything that looks like a box. Label the pet bag in large letters. The same rule applies to passports and jewellery: they travel with you, not as part of house removal services.

## Moving day: doors, vans and escape routes

Your home will have open front doors, a ramp, and furniture sliding through hallways. That is a nightmare for a terrier and an invitation for an indoor cat.

The simplest trick is to get pets out of the property before [Warrior Removals](/house-removals-wigan/) arrives. A friend, a day of kennels or cattery, or a long stay in the car with a helper all work. If that is impossible, use one closed room the crew will not enter, with water, litter and a sign on the door. Tell the foreman at the walkthrough.

Do not “let them say goodbye” in the garden while the van is open. Do not shut a cat in a bedroom that still needs emptying. Coordinate, then leave the loading and unloading to the crew.

## How they travel between Wigan addresses

Most local jobs are short: Swinley to Standish, Ince to Aspull, Leigh to Atherton. Even ten minutes is too long for an unrestrained animal.

Dogs should be in a crate or a harness clipped to a seatbelt. Cats stay in a latched carrier, towel over the top for calm, ventilation clear. Do not open the carrier on the new driveway “so they can see the house”. That is the classic indoor-cat disappearing act.

Rabbits and guinea pigs travel in secure carriers in the car, out of sun and heater blast. Empty hutches can go on the van once cleaned; animals do not ride inside them.

## First night in the new house

Set up one quiet room before you unpack the kitchen: familiar bedding, water, litter tray or puppy pads, and a hiding place. Let cats own that room first. Walk the garden and check fences before dogs go out, terraces around Scholes, Pemberton and the town centre often share alleys and low walls.

Keep feeding and walk times close to the old routine. A known bowl on the floor settles a dog faster than a perfectly placed sofa. Spend a couple of days on quiet routes (Mesnes Park, canal paths, lanes around Shevington) before a busy high-street walk.

## A short pet-friendly checklist

- Book a survey and a fixed quote early
- Update tags, chip and vet records
- Pack a pet bag that stays with you
- Arrange day care or a sealed safe room
- Transport animals in the car
- Set up one room at the new house first
- Check fences and windows before unsupervised access
