Choosing a removals company is the decision that decides whether moving day feels controlled or chaotic. Price matters, but the cheapest number is often a different product: no survey, vague insurance, and a van that is too small. If you are hiring [house removal services in Wigan](/house-removals-wigan/), use a short, boring checklist. Boring checklists prevent expensive Saturdays.

## Start with a survey and a written quote

A reputable firm will look at the job, in person or by video, before locking a price. They need volume, stairs, parking, lofts and anything that must come apart. Phone quotes based on “it’s a three-bed” miss the American fridge on the second floor and the yellow lines outside a terrace in Scholes.

Ask for the quote in writing, with what is included: crew size, van type, packing, dismantling and reassembly, and how loading and unloading are handled at both addresses. Use a [moving cost calculator](/moving-cost-calculator/) only as a range. The survey is the figure you compare.

## Insurance in actual numbers, not adjectives

“Fully insured” is not a number. Ask for:

- **Goods in transit** per load
- **Public liability**
- What is excluded (jewellery, documents, self-packed fragiles, hazardous goods)

Our cover is £50,000 goods in transit per vehicle load and £1 million public liability, described in our terms. Fragile breakage claims typically require professional packing. If a company cannot put figures in writing, you are guessing who pays for a damaged banister or a smashed television.

That is one reason to avoid a pure man-and-van-only arrangement with no paperwork. You want a domestic removals crew that wraps, straps and inventories the risk, not a cash day-rate with a borrowed Transit.

## Services that match your move

List what you actually need:

- House move only, or packing as well
- Beds and wardrobes dismantled and rebuilt
- Office contents mixed with a home
- A local WN-to-WN job versus a Greater Manchester run

Then ask whether the company does those things. Warrior Removals is a removals company: packing, loading, transit and unloading of household and office belongings. We are **not** waste clearance, **not** a storage operator, and **not** an international shipper. If you need those, hire specialists separately rather than forcing them into a house quote.

Check they cover your postcodes. Our [areas](/areas/) pages show the Wigan and nearby neighbourhoods we work regularly, from Standish and Shevington to Leigh, Hindley and Ashton-in-Makerfield.

## Local knowledge and access

Wigan moves fail on access more often than on mileage. Town-centre bays, terraced streets, and flats above shops need a crew that has planned parking and carry distances. Ask how they handle Wigan Council dispensations, tight stairs and long carries. A company based at 61-69 Standishgate, working 7am-9pm seven days a week, can usually shape the day around completion times and lift bookings.

Ask who is on the van. The same team should load and unload. Belongings should not vanish into a depot you never see.

## Reviews, contactability and terms

Read recent Google reviews for specifics: punctuality, careful wrapping, communication when timings slipped. A 5.0 rating is a signal; the comments tell you whether sofas were placed in the right rooms.

You should be able to speak to someone on 01942 565786 or email info@wiganremoval.co.uk and get a survey booked without a hard sell. Read the terms: cancellation, waiting time, what you must disconnect, and how claims work. Professionalism shows up there, not in slogans.

Do not choose on a fabricated history you cannot verify. Choose on the quote, the insurance, the survey and how they talk about your actual staircase.

## Punctuality on the day

Ask for an arrival window, a named contact, and what happens if the previous job overruns. Confirm they will walk through both properties, protect floors, and not leave until you have checked placement and visible condition.

A reliable crew arrives with blankets, straps, stretch wrap and the right number of people. That is house removal services. A late, under-staffed van is how DIY packing gets blamed for damage that started with a rushed load.

## A one-page hiring checklist

- Survey completed (video or in person)
- Fixed written quote, inclusions listed
- GIT and PL figures in writing
- Packing and dismantling priced if needed
- Same crew door to door
- Not a waste, storage or “cash van only” job
- Parking and access discussed
- Contact details and terms you have actually read
