Moving **to** Wigan is a different planning job from shuffling three streets across town. You may not have seen the new hallway with a tape measure, the parking at both ends, or how a 3.5-tonne Luton sits on a WN1 terrace. A “perfect” house move is not luck. It is a survey, a packing plan, utilities timed to keys, and a crew that does loading and unloading as one piece of work.

If you are coming from elsewhere in Greater Manchester or from further afield in the UK, this is the sequence that keeps completion day civilised.

## Get the property facts before you book the van

As soon as the offer is accepted or the tenancy is signed, collect:

- Completion or key-handover time
- Floor level, lift, parking and any concierge rules
- What stays (oven, carpets, curtains) and what you are bringing
- Photos of awkward corners, loft hatches and garden access

Send those to your removal company. Warrior Removals will still want an in-person or video survey of **both** addresses where possible. A video walkthrough of the Wigan property works well if you have not collected keys yet; a survey of the origin house is what sizes the load.

Use the [moving cost calculator](/moving-cost-calculator/) for a steer, then treat the written quote as the plan. [House removal services](/house-removals-wigan/) priced without seeing volume are how people end up with a second trip.

## Learn the neighbourhood on paper, then on foot

Wigan is not one kind of house. Town centre and Swinley mean Mesnes Park on the doorstep and tighter parking. Standish, Shevington, Orrell and Winstanley are more likely to offer drives. Leigh, Hindley, Atherton and Ashton-in-Makerfield have their own high streets, schools and rush-hour patterns.

Browse our [areas](/areas/) pages for the neighbourhood you are buying or renting into, then walk it once if you can: bin day clues, school-run congestion, alleyways behind terraces. That walk is also when you spot whether a Luton can stand outside or whether you need a Wigan Council parking dispensation.

## Pack with the new rooms in mind

Label boxes for the **new** house, not the old one. “Bedroom 2, Wigan” is useless if Bedroom 2 changes meaning. Use the floor plan: front reception, rear kitchen, first-floor back bedroom.

Pack non-essentials early. Keep a first-night box: bedding, kettle, chargers, toilet roll, snacks, tools, medication. Carry passports, cash and jewellery yourself. We do not transport those, and we do not take waste you meant to throw before you left.

If the origin house is a long drive from Standishgate, book packing at that end so moving day is transport, not a 6am boxing panic. Professional packing also supports fragile cover under our terms; self-packed glass is moved carefully but is not a breakage claim.

## Utilities, council and the boring admin

Give energy, water and broadband providers the move date as soon as it is firm. Photograph meter readings at both properties. Register for council tax with Wigan Council if you are coming into the borough; if you are staying in Greater Manchester but changing district, both councils need the dates.

Set up Royal Mail redirection from the old address. Update banks, insurers, DVLA, GP and schools. None of that is the crew’s job, but doing it late is how a smooth unloading still feels like a bad move.

We work 7am-9pm, seven days a week, which helps when solicitors release keys at lunchtime rather than 9am.

## Moving day when you are the one arriving

Be at the Wigan property before the van, or have someone with keys who can direct rooms. Walk the crew through: which bedroom is which, where the sofa lives, which boxes are “open first”. Check floors, then let loading and unloading run.

Inspect furniture before the paperwork is signed. Take new meter photos. Make beds first. Eat something. The “perfect” move is one where you can sleep that night, not one where every picture is hung by midnight.

Cover on the van is £50,000 goods in transit per vehicle load and £1 million public liability. The same crew that loaded you should unload you; your belongings should not sit in a storage unit we do not operate. This is a door-to-door domestic removal, not a man-and-van-only hop and not an international shipment.

- **Eight weeks**: survey booked, declutter started  
- **Four weeks**: packing materials in, utilities notified, parking thought through  
- **One week**: first-night box, freezer defrosted, quote reconfirmed  
- **Day**: keys, walkthrough, load, travel, unload, beds, kettle
