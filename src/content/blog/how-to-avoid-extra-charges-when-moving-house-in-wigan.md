Extra charges on a house move rarely come from nowhere. They usually come from a quote that never matched the job: volume guessed over the phone, a van that cannot park on a Wigan terrace, or a crew standing idle while cupboards are still full. If you want a predictable bill for domestic removals, the work happens before moving day, not in an argument on the driveway.

Here is how extra costs typically arise on Wigan moves, and how to stop them.

## Get a surveyed, written price, not a hopeful figure

The cheapest way to “avoid extras” is to stop comparing numbers that are not the same product. Ask every company whether the price is fixed after seeing the property. An in-person or video survey is how [house removal services](/house-removals-wigan/) should be priced: bedrooms, lofts, sheds, stairs, parking and dismantling all visible.

Use the [moving cost calculator](/moving-cost-calculator/) for a range. Treat it as a range. The survey turns it into a written quote for packing (if booked), loading and unloading, crew size and van loads.

If a firm will only quote by the hour with no survey, you are buying open-ended labour. Narrow streets in Scholes, Ince and the town centre, plus long carries from a second-floor flat, eat hours. A fixed quote after survey puts that risk on the company, provided you described the job honestly.

## Tell the truth about volume and awkward items

Extras appear when a piano, treadmill, American fridge or loft full of boxes was never mentioned. We would rather hear it at survey than invent space in a 3.5-tonne Luton on the day.

Declutter before the survey if you can. Less volume is the cleanest saving. Sell, donate or arrange separate disposal. Warrior Removals is not a waste clearance service: bags of rubbish and broken furniture for dumping do not belong on a household van and are not “included if there is room”.

## Access and parking are not small print

Wigan has easy driveways in Standish, Winstanley and Hawkley Hall, and it has yellow lines, town-centre bays and terraces where a Luton cannot legally stop outside the door. A parking ticket, a 40-metre carry, or waiting for a neighbour to move a car all cost time.

If you need a Wigan Council parking dispensation, apply early. At survey, point out:

- Restricted parking at both addresses, common across [Wigan neighbourhoods](/areas/)
- Flights of stairs and whether there is a working lift
- Narrow halls, tight turns and loft hatches
- Shared drives and time limits on bays near Standishgate

Honest access notes keep the quote accurate. Surprises on the day are what generate extras with operators who priced a fantasy job.

## Be ready when the crew arrives

A quote assumes the house is prepared: freezer defrosted, appliances disconnected as required, paths clear, last-minute packing finished. If the team is wrapping a sofa while you are still filling boxes, the day stretches.

Pack early or book packing as part of the same quote. Label rooms so unloading is not a guessing game. Keep jewellery, cash and documents with you, they are not part of the van inventory.

Warrior Removals prices Saturday and Sunday the same as weekdays in the fixed quote. You do not need to dodge the weekend to avoid a “premium”, but you may still prefer a midweek slot if every completion in the street is on the last Friday of the month.

## Compare insurance, not just the headline

A low cash price from a man-and-van-only outfit looks attractive until a wardrobe corner goes through a plaster wall or a box of glasses arrives as shards. Confirm **goods in transit** and **public liability** in writing. Ours are £50,000 per vehicle load and £1 million public liability, set out in our terms.

Fragile breakage cover depends on who packed the item. Professionally packed kitchenware is treated differently from a supermarket carton you taped shut at midnight. That is not a hidden extra; it is how cover works. If the china matters, budget for packing rather than hoping.

We do not sell storage months or international freight as part of a local move, so you will not find those sitting underneath a “from” price either.

## A practical anti-extras checklist

- Book a free survey, then a written fixed quote
- Declare lofts, sheds, outbuildings and large appliances
- Arrange parking or a dispensation before moving day
- Declutter and dispose of waste separately
- Finish packing or book packers
- Disconnect appliances on time
- Keep valuables with you
- Reconfirm date, access and inventory a few days out

Do that, and the invoice should match the quote you already accepted.
